About Me

Where Curiosity Meets Strategy & Analytical Rigor

MBA Marketing Candidate at IILM University, Greater Noida · Physics Graduate.

I like understanding why people buy things, what makes a brand memorable, and how a good idea becomes a great campaign. Naturally, I decided to study marketing after graduating in Physics—because apparently, understanding the universe wasn't enough; I also wanted to understand consumers.

I'm currently pursuing an MBA in Marketing at IILM University, Greater Noida, building my skills across marketing strategy, consumer behaviour, data analytics, and creative communication.

My background in Physics taught me to question assumptions, analyse problems, and look beyond the obvious. I'm now applying that analytical mindset to marketing—combining data-driven thinking with creative ideas to understand audiences and solve business problems.

I'm particularly interested in creative campaign planning, brand strategy, marketing analytics, consumer insights, and the intersection of creativity and technology. Outside the classroom, I enjoy exploring marketing trends, researching ideas, developing portfolio projects, and writing about how marketing works in the real world.

"My goal is simple: create marketing that doesn't just look good, but makes sense, connects with people, and delivers measurable results. Ideally, all three at once."
